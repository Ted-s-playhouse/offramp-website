Gut-check for the data-driven investors here 👇

I'm building a community called Off-ramp — a group of real estate investors who run on data instead of guesswork. And I built the app to back it up, because I got tired of how scattered pre-foreclosure work is: the auction list in one place, skip tracing in another, obituaries and court records somewhere else, and the lender quietly postponing a sale you never find out about. Off-ramp puts the whole thing in one place.

What the app actually does for you:
• Finds the highest-priority, highest-equity foreclosures in your area — with loan type + property detail already attached
• Gives you skip-traced contact info so you can actually reach the owner
• Auto-scrapes distress signals on each lead: obituaries, divorce, bankruptcy, incarceration
• Skip traces next of kin + nearby neighbors so you've got real people to call about the property
• Watches the foreclosing lender's site and flags postponements + cancellations in real time
• Push notifications the second a file changes — you never miss a move
• Keeps every lead, note, and status in one place so you work the deal instead of rebuilding the file every time

Straight up on the limits: death records only report ~70% of the time, and some attorney sites don't post clean postponement statuses — no tool fixes that. But everything else lives in one dashboard.
