What it is

OffRamp REI is a deal-sourcing and deal-management platform for real-estate investors. It continuously pulls pre-auction foreclosures and other distressed situations from every county in the country, cleans and de-duplicates them, enriches each one with equity math and skip-traced contact info, layers on AI + human-verified insight, then ranks them so you spend your time on the handful of deals that actually pencil — not the thousands that don't.

The core problem it solves: foreclosure data is scattered across 3,000+ county systems, paid listing sites, and auction platforms — each in a different format, most of it stale, duplicated, or missing the one thing you need: a way to reach the owner before the sale. OffRamp collapses all of that into a single, current, contactable, prioritized pipeline.

How it works — the pipeline

  1. Ingest — cast the widest net

    Automated feeds pull new distress signals every day from county recorder data, foreclosure listing sites, live auction platforms, distress data providers, and off-market wholesaler feeds. Nothing is typed by hand.

  2. Normalize & de-duplicate

    Every source formats addresses, names, and dates differently. OffRamp standardizes them to one schema and merges duplicates by parcel/address so a single property never shows up five times from five sources.

  3. Enrich — turn a record into a deal

    Each property is enriched with ownership, loan balances, recorded liens, estimated value, and equity position — plus skip-traced owner contact (mobile phones, emails, heirs) so it's immediately actionable.

  4. Insight — AI + VA verification

    An AI layer reads the situation and flags distress signals, condition clues, and priority. A human VA layer verifies the highest-value leads and adds context a database can't — the "unique insight" edge that separates a real deal from a data point.

  5. Score & prioritize

    Every lead gets ranked by the combination that matters: high equity + high motivation + reachable owner + time-to-sale. You open the app to a sorted list of the best opportunities, not a spreadsheet dump.

  6. Work it — the built-in CRM

    Call, text, track status, log outreach, and push a lead from "found" to "under contract" without leaving the platform. Push notifications fire when a high-priority deal lands in your market.
