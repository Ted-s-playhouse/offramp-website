4. What makes it sticky (ranked by how hard it is to leave)

  • Their data lives here. Every skip trace, note, call outcome and postcard sent stays on the lead. Leaving means losing the contact history. Build: pipeline stages + notes on the card (the Engage section already has the hooks).
  • Daily alert email. "4 new pre-foreclosures in Utah County since yesterday" with photos. Zero cost, keeps the tab open every morning. Build: saved search + one cron.
  • Use-it-or-lose-it allowances. Skip traces and postcards reset monthly. People do not cancel with 18 postcards left.
  • Postcard autopilot. "Send a postcard to every new lead in my saved search" as a toggle. Turns a subscription into a mail service they would otherwise set up elsewhere.
  • Annual plan. 2 months free on annual. Locks 12 months of revenue and cuts churn math in half.
  • Seats. Team tier makes the acquisition manager and the caller share one pipeline. Two people using it is much stickier than one.
  • Public pages as the funnel. Every SEO page shows the photo and the sale date and locks the owner. The upgrade is one tap away from Google.
  • Win tracking. "Deals closed from OffRamp leads" counter on the Account tab. The number they would be throwing away.
