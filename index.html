The simple version

How you make money — and help someone out

No jargon. Here's the whole idea in four steps.

1

A homeowner is about to lose everything

They fell behind on their mortgage. In a few weeks the county sells their house at a foreclosure auction — and if it sells, they lose the home and every dollar of equity they built up.

2

You find them before the auction

OffRamp shows you these homes early — with what they owe, what the house is actually worth (the equity), and the owner's phone number.

3

You solve their problem

You offer to buy the house before it goes to auction. They walk away with cash in their pocket instead of nothing — and skip the foreclosure on their credit.

4

You make money on the difference

Because you bought it for less than it's worth, you keep the gap. Resell it for a profit, or hold it as a rental. Everyone wins.

Six ways into every deal

Foreclosure is only the start. The same life events that push a home toward auction — divorce, bankruptcy, and death among them — create equity opportunities long before the sale. OffRamp tracks all six from the first filing to sale day, so you're never late to the equity.

🏛️

Pre-Foreclosure

Notices of default and trustee-sale filings — the owner still has a cure window and real equity to protect.

🔨

Auction

Upcoming trustee and sheriff's sales with the posted opening bid — the total debt, and the fastest read on equity.

📜

Probate

Estates working through the courts, where an heir or personal representative needs to sell before the equity slips away.

💔

Divorce

Splitting households often means selling the marital home fast — a motivated, time-pressured seller with equity on the line.

⚖️

Bankruptcy

Chapter 7 and 13 filings surface owners restructuring debt — often needing to sell or settle a property quickly.

🕊️

Death

A deceased owner leaves heirs facing taxes, upkeep, and a mortgage they didn't plan for — the classic estate-sale opportunity.

Divorce, bankruptcy, and death feed directly into pre-foreclosure and probate — OffRamp tracks the whole chain, so you reach the seller at the first sign, not the last.

Questions, answered

The stuff investors actually ask before they sign up.

What do I get on the free plan?
100 full property lookups a month — verified equity, loan balance, foreclosure/auction status, sale date, and estate/probate flags — with no credit card. The one thing gated to Pro is skip-traced owner contact (phone/email) and the field/farming tools, because each contact lookup carries a real data cost.
How does the 7-day trial work?
Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. You add a card, get full access, and if you don't cancel it rolls into your plan — founding rate $49/mo (or $588/yr) while spots last, otherwise the standard $149/mo. Cancel any time from the self-serve billing portal — no phone call, no email required.
What's the founding-member deal?
The first 100 Pro subscribers lock $49/mo (or $588 for the year — that's $49/mo, save $900 vs standard annual) for a full 12 months, then renew at standard pricing. When the 100 spots are gone, founding pricing closes for good. The counter at the top of pricing shows how many are left.
How do skip-traces work on Pro?
Pro includes 250 skip-traces a month — that's pulling verified mobile, email, and DNC status on an owner (property lookups themselves are unlimited and don't count). Need more? Overage is $0.20 each, or grab a 500-pack for $79. Skip-traced contact data is consumable, so used credits aren't refundable.
What's your refund policy?
Property and contact lookups are consumable — each one is a live paid data pull — so charges for used lookups aren't refundable. You can cancel any time and keep access through the end of your paid period. No surprise renewals: we email you before every charge.
Where does the data come from — and how current is it?
Reports are reconciled from county records, trustee/auction postings, and licensed property + contact data, refreshed daily. Foreclosure and auction status pull live at the moment you search, so a days-to-auction countdown is always current.
Is this legal? What can I use the contact data for?
Yes — for legitimate real-estate and permissible business purposes. Skip-traced contact information is provided under DPPA/GLBA permissible-use rules; you'll confirm your permissible purpose before contact details are shown, and you're responsible for TCPA/DNC compliance when you reach out. It is not for credit, employment, insurance, or tenant screening.
Do you cover my market?
Yes — OffRamp covers all 50 states nationwide, with verified foreclosure, auction, and probate data everywhere we operate.
