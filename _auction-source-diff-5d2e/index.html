Auction sources side by side — 2026-10-03

REAPI auction = properties with a county-recorded notice of sale (PropertySearch auction:true, free count mode, no records billed). Auction.com live = listings in the public auction.com feed with status ACTIVE and not delisted (our crm.offramp_national_listings table).

Why REAPI is roughly double. REAPI counts every trustee and sheriff sale notice on the county record in all 51 jurisdictions. Auction.com is one platform's inventory. They overlap only about a third: in a free 150-address test of live auction.com listings, REAPI knew 148 but flagged auction on 51, pre-foreclosure-only on 36, and neither on 61 (bank-owned and online auctions carry no fresh county notice). Neither source contains the other. The real auction universe is the union with address dedupe.
