Highest-margin stack in one line

Discovery: CourtListener RECAP (free/$10/mo) + Apify docket actor ($0.0036/record) for bankruptcy; REAPI PropertySearch distress filters + DealMachine lien_doc_type filters (both already paid, ~$0.005-$0.01/record) for liens/judgments; free county recorder + Idaho SOS scrapers for same-day Mountain West filings. Enrichment: REAPI PropertyDetail (~$0.0103/record) for equity/mortgage; REAPI Involuntary Liens ($0.75) only post-screen. Contact: Tracerfy $0.02/hit + $0.02 DNC scrub for volume, REAPI $0.05 (DNC flag native) for compliance-sensitive pulls. Fully loaded cost of a screened, address-joined, skip-traced bankruptcy or lien lead is roughly $0.05-$0.10 (with the $0.75 lien report: ~$0.85). Comparable retail: Chapter13Leads sells $99-$399/mo per metro, Lispend $199-$249/mo, Skip Trace Depot $0.22/row, attorney lead networks $15-$200/lead - so even a $0.50-$1.00/lead resale price is a 5-20x gross margin. Biggest cost lever: stop running the $0.75 lien report pre-screen; biggest moat lever: the county-recorder scrapers (no vendor resells same-day ID/UT/MT filings).

Fully loaded cost of one callable lead on this stack: discovery $0.001–$0.014, append $0 marginal (inside the REAPI pool), photo $0.007 once, skip trace $0.02–$0.05 on tap. Roughly 3 to 8 cents per lead that a Pro user pays $49 a month or $1+ per tap to see. Margin stays above 90 percent as long as skip trace is never bulk-run.

Bankruptcy filings (Chapter 7 and 13)

Recommendation: Build, don't buy. Backfill from CourtListener bulk CSV ($0, quarterly) and take daily deltas from the CourtListener RECAP API (free at 125 req/day; $10/mo Tier 1 = 300/day, or a commercial agreement) or the Apify parseforge actor ($3.62 per 1,000 dockets, no rate cap). That gives debtor name + chapter + court + filing date within ~1 day of filing. Join debtor name to the property address via REAPI PropertySearch by owner name (~$0.0103/record on our Growth plan, 'count'/'ids_only' free) - so a bankruptcy lead with address costs about $0.014 all-in. Only pull Schedule A/B PDFs via PACER ($0.10/page, $3 cap, free under $30/qtr) for leads that survive the equity screen. Cheapest BUY alternatives: LeadsPlease $0.125/record (weekly, chapter + homeowner flag) or Exact Data $0.21/record (compiled). PropertyRadar ($119/mo + $0.02/export; API at $599/mo) is the only turnkey nationwide source that pre-joins bankruptcy chapter/district to a property address with equity, and is the fallback if we don't want to run the name-match pipeline. Avoid UniCourt (~$2,250/mo API), Docket Alarm ($4/doc), Westlaw/Lexis (law-firm pricing). RealEstateAPI has no bankruptcy field.

Liens, judgments, lis pendens, tax liens

Recommendation: Two-tier. Tier 1 (discovery, cheap): REAPI PropertySearch distress filters (judgment, tax_lien, pre_foreclosure, foreclosure) are already included in our ~$0.0103/record Growth-plan pool - use them to find candidates for free-ish, and reserve the $0.75/match Involuntary Liens report for leads that pass the equity screen (it's the most expensive per-record item in our stack after CoreLogic's reported $11.50/call and DataTree's $7.03/report). DealMachine (already integrated, ~$0.0047/credit on our plan) exposes lien_doc_type incl. HOA LIEN, MECHANICS LIEN, JUDGMENT, LIS PENDENS, FEDERAL/STATE TAX LIEN plus total_open_lien_amount - the cheapest lien-TYPED record in the stack and the first place to pull from. Tier 2 (freshness, home markets): scrape free county recorder indexes (Ada, Bannock, Bonneville ID; Utah County UT; Silver Bow MT) plus Idaho SOS state/IRS lien feeds ($30-$120/yr + $8/file, daily increments) and Salt Lake County data units ($0.03/unit) - same-day lis pendens/judgments nobody else resells. For a turnkey nationwide API with lien-type filters, PropertyRadar Business ($599/mo, ~$0.01/export) beats ATTOM/CoreLogic/DataTree (quote-only, $7-$11.50 per lien call). Benchmarks for what OffRamp can charge: Lispend $199-$249/mo (FL/CA), Chapter13Leads $99-$399/mo per metro, Skip Trace Depot $0.22/row lis pendens with phones.
