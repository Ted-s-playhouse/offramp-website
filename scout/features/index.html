Proprietary data & lead scoring

The edge that's ours, not rented — reconciled data and scoring models you won't find in a stock list.

Reconciled property data

Live

Every lookup is one verified record, reconciled across county records, trustee postings, and licensed data — including the USDA/HUD and junior liens other tools silently drop. A Carfax for the house, not a raw list.

Just say: “Give me the verified equity on 123 Main.”

Motivated-seller scoring

Live

Our own model ranks owners by how likely they are to sell — blending equity, distress stage, auction timing, and life-events (pre-foreclosure, auction, probate, bankruptcy, deceased-owner) into a single 0–100 motivation score, so you work the hottest leads first.

Just say: “Build me a motivated-seller list in Utah, sorted by score.”

Data that compounds

Live

Every property and owner is verified once and cached, so the verified property dataset gets cleaner, deeper, and de-duplicated as the network grows — an edge that builds over time instead of resetting with every new list.

Built in · you get the benefit automatically
