Scout, answered

The things people ask before they start using it.

Where do I talk to Scout?
Right in your chat — no separate app to download. You message Scout like a teammate and it replies in seconds. If you're already in OffRamp, it's the same account and the same data.
Does Scout make up answers?
No. Scout pulls from real county records, trustee/auction postings, and licensed property + contact data. When a fact can't be verified it says so — it won't fabricate an owner, a phone number, or an equity figure.
Can it really run my deal numbers?
Yes — fix-and-flip, BRRRR, buy-and-hold rental, and wholesale assignment. Give it the ARV, rehab, rate, and any payoff, and it returns the max allowable offer and the key metrics (equity, cash-flow, cap rate) so you know your ceiling before you call.
What happens when I paste a link?
Scout fetches it — a YouTube transcript or an article's text — distills the key tactics and numbers into a clean note, and files it into your memory. Later you can ask Scout about it and it'll remember. Public/authorized content only; it won't scrape a private feed.
Is my data private?
Yes. The deals, owners, and notes you work with Scout go into your private memory — other members don't see them. Scout also draws on a shared library of general real-estate knowledge, but that's know-how, not anyone's private pipeline.
Do I have to learn commands?
No. Talk to Scout in plain English. Not sure what it can do? Just ask it "what can you do?" and it'll walk you through it right there in the chat.
