OffRamp REI — Desktop deal room Mockup for sign-off · Phase 5 #28

Static mockup, no app changes. Same palette, cards, toolbar, sheets and section order as the live mobile deal room (/app/). Approve and we build it 1:1; below 900px the app keeps the existing mobile layout untouched.

What you are approving

  • Two independent panes at ≥1100px. Left: the pinned toolbar (search · state chip · Filter) over a scrolling lead list. Right: the selected lead's detail, scrolling on its own, in the mobile order Contact → Evaluation → Court records → Engage → Foreclosure → Property → Analyzer, with free-tier lock rows kept.
  • Engage action bar is sticky at the bottom of the detail pane (Save · Contacted · Hide · Call owner) so it never scrolls away; note, map links and flags stay in the Engage section.
  • Nav moves to a slim left rail (Deals · Map · Search · Account). Why the rail and not the toolbar: the toolbar's right side is already taken by the state chip and Filter, and a rail keeps all four destinations in one fixed spot at every width without crowding search.

Two open questions before building

  1. Default selection on load: auto-open the first lead in the right pane, or show an empty "pick a deal" panel until you click one? (Auto-open costs one lead fetch per list load.)
  2. Map on desktop: should Map replace the detail pane (list stays on the left, pins on the right, click a pin to open detail in place) or stay a full-width screen like mobile?
